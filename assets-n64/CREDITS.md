# Extra music in the N64 port

These songs are generated at build time by `tools/n64_tetris_music.py` and are
included only in the N64 build. They are also credited on the game's Credits screen.

## Korobeiniki (`GAME_g_korobeiniki`, `GAME_h_korobeiniki_turbo`)

The melody is *Korobeiniki*, a 19th-century Russian folk song (public domain),
best known as the Tetris "Type A" theme. The arrangement (chords, bass line,
drums and instruments) was written for this port and does not use the Game Boy
arrangement. It is released under the same license as Apotris.

## Menuet from French Suite No. 3, BWV 814 (`GAME_i_bach_menuet`)

Composed by Johann Sebastian Bach (1722), best known as the Tetris "Type C" theme.

The notes come from the Mutopia Project transcription of the French Suite No. 3,
entered by Knute Snortum (source: Bach-Gesellschaft, 1863), licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/):
<https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=100>

`bach-french-suite-3-menuet.mid` is the unmodified MIDI file from that
transcription. The chiptune arrangement made from it is likewise licensed under
CC BY-SA 4.0.
